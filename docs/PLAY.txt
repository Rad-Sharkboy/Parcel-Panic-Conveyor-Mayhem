Parcel Panic: Conveyor Mayhem V1

Windows 11, 64-bit | 1360 x 765 window
Extract the whole ZIP, then open ParcelPanic.exe.
Keep the assets folder beside the EXE. No compiler is needed to play.

Left click: switch a cyan junction
Right click: brake a belt temporarily
Hover a parcel: show its information
Space / Esc: pause or resume
Z while paused: undo a switch
H: Help / Factory info
Q: view another input queue
F1 / F2 / F3: queue / heap / hash samples (footer buttons also work)
Enter in a sample: next operation; Backspace: restart; Esc: close
F4 / Shift+F4: next / previous level
F5: restart

Match R/B/Y parcels to their docks. X goes to recycling.
F means fragile; ! means express. Reach both the quota and score target.

V1 is the first public release. Future versions may refine the game.
